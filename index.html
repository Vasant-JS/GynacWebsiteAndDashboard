Clear Clinical Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to questions regarding patient registration, follow-up linkage, phone consultations, and hospital visit coordination.

You can plan your consultation using the interactive tool above by picking your category and doctor. After clicking "Continue to Patient Portal," verify your phone number, review doctor availability, and confirm your preferred slot.
Select "Follow-Up Consultation" on the planning tool and provide your previously assigned Sumohan Case ID or the registered phone number. The hospital medical desk cross-references your physical and digital patient chart prior to the call.
We support PDF documents and high-resolution JPG/PNG images up to 10MB each. All uploaded investigations and health declarations are stored on encrypted medical servers accessible strictly by the attending medical practitioner and hospital staff.
Phone consultations are conducted manually via mobile phone with the physician. At your scheduled slot, the hospital desk or attending doctor contacts your registered phone number directly, eliminating complex application downloads.
During the current platform booking phase, all consultation fees and billing are handled directly with the hospital reception. There are no hidden digital convenience markups; payment options are provided when confirming your appointment.
If you need to reschedule or cancel your consultation, please call the hospital reception at +91 86188 18012 or +91 94816 05619 at least 2 hours in advance so our doctors can accommodate waiting emergency maternity cases.
