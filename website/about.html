About Sumohan Care

Sumohan Care supports consultation booking, patient records and follow-up care for Sumohan Medico Surgical Centre and Maternity Home in Shivamogga. The hospital began in 1984 and has served the community for more than four decades, with maternity and child health at the centre of its work.

Vision

Familiar, compassionate healthcare with a continuing commitment to healing, building on the hospital's founding vision.

Mission

Improve patients' health and well-being through responsible care, clinical expertise and high standards of medical practice.

Our Healthcare Approach

The hospital provides obstetrics and gynecology, pediatrics, surgery, medicine and anesthesiology, supported by inpatient and outpatient services, a laboratory, pharmacy and neonatal facilities.

Patient Care

Prepare your patient profile, choose your consultation, share relevant reports, and keep your consultation history available for follow-up. Your treating doctor maintains the clinical case sheet.
