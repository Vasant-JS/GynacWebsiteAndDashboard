Patient Privacy & Consent

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Information used for your care

Registration details, consultation reasons, uploaded reports, prescriptions and clinical case sheets are stored to support appointments and follow-up care. The treating care team can review the patient's consultation history. Administrative staff manage accounts and enquiries.

Your declaration

Before booking, acknowledge medical-information use for consultation and confirm that your information is accurate. The patient name, declaration date and time, and policy version are saved with the booking.

Access and corrections

You can view and update your patient profile and upload relevant reports. Contact sumohanhospital@gmail.com or the hospital phone numbers below for questions about your records or requests to correct them. Clinical edits retain saved revisions so the care team can review changes.

External services

Live payment, messaging and video-call integrations are deferred. This website uses external font and style services; parent-hospital and maps links open external sites.

Using the consultation service

Provide accurate patient information, select an available appointment, and review your declarations before booking. Use your patient account for appointment changes and follow-up records. Contact the hospital to confirm fees and consultation arrangements; online payment and call integrations are not active yet.

Patient information and support

You can review your profile, appointments, reports and prescriptions in your patient account. Ask your care team to explain your consultation and treatment plan, and contact the hospital about corrections to your records or support needs.
