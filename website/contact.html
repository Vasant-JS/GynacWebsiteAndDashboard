Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a consultation?

Register as a patient, log in with your mobile number, choose a consultation category and doctor, select an available slot, and acknowledge the patient declarations.

How do I book a follow-up?

Select Follow-Up Consultation and choose your previous visit. Your consultation history and reports remain linked to your account.

Can I upload reports?

Yes. PDF, JPG and PNG reports can be uploaded while booking. You can also manage reports in your patient account.

How do I attend my consultation?

Contact the hospital to confirm the consultation arrangements. Video/audio call integration is not active yet.

How do I pay?

Online payment integration is not active yet. No payment is collected by the current booking flow. Contact the hospital to confirm fees and payment arrangements.

Can I change my appointment?

Use the appointments page to view your booking and available cancellation or rescheduling options.

What if I cannot log in or upload a file?

Check that you are using your registered mobile number and a supported file under 10 MB. Contact the hospital team if you need help.
